    Controls

    • Tap a unit or building to select it. Double-tap a unit to select all of that type on screen.
    • With units selected, tap the ground to move, a resource to gather, an enemy to attack, or a foundation to build. Tap the ground to move. Tap ✖ Deselect to clear the selection.
    • Drag to move the camera, pinch to zoom. Press and hold, then drag, to box-select (or use ⬚).
    • Side buttons: 👷 next idle villager, ⚔️ select your army, ⬚ box select, 🏰 jump to your Town Center.

    Your first minutes

    • Keep your Town Center making Villagers all game.
    • Send villagers to berries and trees. Build a Mill by the berries and a Lumber Camp by the forest so they walk less.
    • Build a House before you run out of population. Each house adds 5.
    • When berries run out, build Farms around your Town Center or Mill.
    • Add a Barracks, Archery Range and Stable, and mine gold to pay for soldiers.

    Winning fights

    • Spearmen beat Knights. Archers beat Swordsmen and Spearmen from range. Knights run down Archers.
    • Swordsmen are best at knocking down buildings.
    • Destroy every enemy building and unit to win.

    Desktop: left-click to select or drag a box, right-click to command, WASD or arrow keys to pan, mouse wheel to zoom.
