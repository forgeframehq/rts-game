How to play

  • Tap a unit or building to select it. Double-tap a unit to select all of that type on screen.
  • With units selected, tap the ground to move, a resource to gather, an enemy to attack, or a foundation to build.
  • Drag to move the camera, pinch to zoom. Press and hold, then drag, to box-select (or use ⬚).
  • Villagers gather food, wood, gold and stone. Build Houses for population, then a Barracks, Archery Range and Stable.
  • Destroy every enemy building and unit to win.
  • Desktop: left-click select or drag a box, right-click to command, WASD or arrow keys to pan, mouse wheel to zoom.
